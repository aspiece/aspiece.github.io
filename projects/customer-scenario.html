03 / Design intent

Why a branching situation works.

  • Context first: each decision occurs within a realistic service interaction rather than an abstract multiple-choice quiz.
  • Meaningful distractors: tempting but incomplete choices help a learner explain what makes a response effective.
  • Immediate feedback: coaching explains the performance principle, not merely a correct/incorrect label.
  • Reflection: the final summary encourages transfer from a scenario to future work.
  • Accessible interactions: keyboard-operable buttons, labeled progress, high contrast, and live-region feedback support broad participation.

Authorship: Original demonstration produced for this portfolio in November 2026. No proprietary customer service manual or vehicle policy was used. Feedback reflects general customer-communication principles rather than employer-specific instructions.
