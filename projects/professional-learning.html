01 / Mentor development

Transfer ownership instead of giving answers.

Mentoring for Innovation combines two completed mentoring presentations into a 15-slide professional development experience, with a five-phase framework, realistic scenario, partner practice and facilitator notes. The original presentations remain archived in the case study.

02 / FIRST Mentor Camp

Use participant feedback to improve professional learning.

Archived mentor-camp planning materials and a completed survey-results presentation document the work of organizing professional learning for robotics mentors. The survey synthesis, co-authored with Ellen Crain, identified requests for more hands-on programming, team sustainability, technical mentoring and practical examples.

The evidence demonstrates a feedback-informed approach to event design. It does not establish measured changes in mentor performance. Individual survey responses and participant information remain private.

03 / Conference presentations

Translate emerging standards into practical decisions.

At MICE Con in October 2026, I presented two sessions: The New CSTA Standards: High School Foundations and Specialty Pathways and AI Literacy for Career-Ready Students. The standards session was structured around foundational expectations, specialty pathways and a concrete planning activity for educators. The AI literacy session connected responsible AI education with career preparation.

The standards presentation adapted materials from NJ CSTA and credits CSTA's 2026 materials under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The original third-party slides are not reproduced in this portfolio.
