03 / Measurement thinking

Different measures answer different questions.

01Reach and participation

How many educators or volunteers attended or accessed the training, and did they finish?

02Learner experience

Did people find the experience useful, understandable, and relevant to their role?

03Application on the job

Could teaching teams use the resources, plan their roles, and start the school year prepared?

04Follow-through

What support requests and performance feedback suggest the need for another revision?

The 2023 summer-training design document explicitly connects audience, duration, objectives, delivery modes, and intended measures for its modules.

04 / Evidence standard

Separate a claim from its proof.

Validation note: The public case studies avoid implying that the reported 50% satisfaction improvement came from the 2023 Classroom Teacher Orientation or the 2021 Volunteer Curriculum Training package. The underlying survey data, baseline definitions, and sample sizes for the résumé figures have not been recovered; those details would be necessary to publish a complete statistical analysis.

The portfolio therefore emphasizes traceable deliverables, an explicit measurement approach, and transparent limits rather than assigning unverified causal results to a course.
