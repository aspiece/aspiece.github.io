Selected work / Volunteer Curriculum Training

Workplace learning · Microsoft TEALS · 2021–2022

Turning curriculum access into volunteer readiness.

A multi-path Storyline experience supporting industry volunteers as they prepare to help teach computer science.

01 / Performance need

Prepare volunteers for the classroom, not just the curriculum.

Microsoft TEALS supported industry volunteers joining high school computer science teaching teams. Volunteers needed to know where to find course materials, understand the scope of the curriculum they would support, and practice technical tasks similar to what students would encounter.

02 / Learning design

A path tailored to the course and learner.

The recovered published training shows an initial decision for returning volunteers versus volunteers new to their course. The 2022 course requirements specify curriculum-specific branches and completion at the end of each branch.

The documented course framework covers a curriculum overview, how to access materials, instructional approaches, a sample syllabus, and a pacing guide. The Intro to CS Snap! branch included hands-on practice such as Pong, a platform game, and a word game, with instructions for submitting work for feedback.

03 / Development & accessibility

Branching logic and LMS-ready thinking.

  • Branch-specific learner paths: route volunteers to the content relevant to the curriculum they will support.
  • Completion rules: the 2022 requirements explicitly call for completion at the end of each branch.
  • Accessible media: the course specification requires captions for prerecorded media and meaningful alternative text for relevant visuals.
  • Consistent production: Microsoft writing and TEALS brand guidance supported a recognizable, coherent experience.
  • Storyline + SCORM: the preserved TEALS project collection includes authored Storyline sources and published LMS packages; this evidence demonstrates hands-on course development and interoperability.

04 / Evidence boundary

What is verified—and what is not.

Reviewed artifacts include a published 2021 Volunteer Curriculum Training ZIP, the 2022 Course Requirements document, and branch-specific content plans for Intro to CS with Python and Snap!. These confirm the planned learner pathways, curriculum tasks, and accessibility expectations.

Evidence boundary: The 2021 visual is not presented as a screenshot of the 2022 source file. The recovered documents establish design intent and project assets, but we have not verified course-specific completion statistics or live LMS tracking results.
